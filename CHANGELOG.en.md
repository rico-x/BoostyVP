## Unreleased — first public release

- Subscriptions, combined New feed, creator pages, per-account history and Continue watching.
- Watch later, favorite videos/creators, watched markers, local search and a session queue.
- Text posts, publication details and video comments without starting playback.
- Native playback: seeking, quality, per-creator speed, signed-link recovery,
  sleep timer, next video, configurable remote buttons and stream information.
- Background pause and position preservation, including restoration of the open player.
- Configurable cards, captions, density, themes and compact menus; expanding the
  sidebar shifts the catalog and the focus preview demonstrates actual changes.
- Library/privacy controls and immediate settings persistence.
- File-manager export, settings import/transfer, Android TV/Projectivy channels
  and manual update checks.
- Russian/English interfaces, guides and current screenshots.
- ARMv7, ARM64, x86, x86_64 and universal builds; signing, checksums, scans
  and build-provenance workflow prepared.

The workflow assigns a date and daily sequence: `YYYY.MM.DD.N`.
